## Long

**en**
When the child falls asleep, the toys wake up, and the Junk Gang crawls out from under the bed! Dust bunnies, runaway marbles, sock bats, wind-up beetle tanks and the Junk King on his vacuum-cleaner throne all want the shiny star sticker in the toy box.

Build tin soldiers, block catapults, bubble blowers, rubber-band rifles and paper-plane hangars on the build pads along the race track, masking-tape line and ruler bridge. Upgrade them, choose one of two specialisations at level 4, and use the bouncy ball and the night light when things get tight.

- 30 levels in 3 worlds: Bedroom floor, Study desk, Backyard sandbox
- 5 toy towers, 10 special upgrades, 7 junk creatures and a boss
- Sticker album with permanent perks, plus an endless mode
- Mouse and touch, no text needed to play
