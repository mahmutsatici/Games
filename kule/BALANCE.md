# Toy Fort — balance

Everything below the results marker is generated by `npm run balance` (bots play the real sim headless: 30 levels × 8 bots × 20 seeds × perks off/on).
Other tools: `npm run calibrate` (per-level enemy HP so the smart bot lands in its lives band), `npm run trace -- <level> <bot>` (one game, wave by wave), `npm run gen` (rebuild level data).

## Tuning log (what changed and why)

1. **Bench method.**
   - The first "damage per gold" bench stopped after 20 leaks, which made every number wrong. Bench runs now get unlimited lives.
   - Damage towers are measured as sustained DPS on an endless stream of their intended target.
   - Bubbles are measured as the extra HP a level-2 catapult removes from a loose marble stream thanks to the slow. A slow does not raise single-target kill rate in a steady stream; its value is bunching enemies for splash and smoothing bursts.
2. **Tower stats** (spec starting values → now), to bring damage per gold within ±20% against intended targets:
   - catapult damage 18 → 14, splash share 60% → 50%
   - rubber band damage 45 → 55
   - hangar damage 14 → 11
   - bubble: cost 120 → 110, rate 1.0 → 1.6, range 2.2 → 2.5, slow 40% → 45%, slow time 1.5 → 2.0 s, and the bubble now bursts into a 0.6-tile cloud (slows everyone inside, damages only the target)
3. **Scaling.** HP +13%/level and +10%/wave made late levels impossible: a level-20 boss would have about 14,000 HP against roughly 1,700 gold of total income. Now HP +6%/level and +6%/wave, bounty +3.5%/level (still slower than HP, as designed). Bosses scale with the level only, not with the last-wave factor.
4. **Wave data.**
   - Budgets are spent in threat units, not raw HP, so swarms stay swarms (a 10-HP marble used to count as a third of a bunny).
   - Marble groups grow per world: 8–12, 10–16, 14–20.
   - From world 2, most waves pair two different threats.
   - Pads also cover the straight flight line of flyers (at least two pads see it).
5. **Armor matters.** Beetles 4 → 8, robots 2 → 4, King 3 → 6, plus 3% per level, so rapid weak hits really are punished.
6. **Per-level calibration.** `calibrate.ts` sets each level's enemy-HP multiplier (`LEVEL_GEN.levelHp`) so the smart bot lands in its lives band. It tunes HP rather than wave size, because smaller waves also mean less gold.
7. **Bots.**
   - The smart bot models tower throughput (a single-target tower can only give each enemy about one spacing gap of its time), overkill, splash/chain/pierce density and 3-wave lookahead.
   - Greedy weights raw DPS by the share of upcoming enemies a tower can hit at all.

## Known open targets (honest status)

- The heuristic smart bot still prefers soldiers and rubber bands. Because difficulty is calibrated to it, `only-soldier` and `only-band` also win most levels, and greedy and naive win too often.
- Next step: give the smart bot a simulation lookahead (copy the sim, try the top candidate builds against the next wave), then recalibrate. A stronger smart bot allows higher difficulty, which single-tower strategies cannot meet.
