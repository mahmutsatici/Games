# Sucuk Hop: store listings

Shared copy for CrazyGames, Poki, GameDistribution, Playgama and itch.io. Each store section lists its own fields.

## Shared copy

**Title:** Sucuk Hop

**Tagline:** Hold to charge, release to hop: help a wobbly sucuk cross a giant kitchen.

**Description:** Meet Sucuk, an elastic Turkish sausage with googly eyes, on a mission through a giant kitchen. Hold to charge a jump, release to hop in the direction it leans, and whichever end lands sticks. Mind the slippery butter, bounce on jelly, and dodge the hot grill, which sends you back to the last checkpoint flag. Reach the hot-dog bun to finish each level, collect spice coins, and earn up to 3 stars by hopping efficiently.

**Controls:** Hold mouse / touch / Space to charge, release to hop.

**Tags:** physics, one button, casual, arcade, skill, platformer, cooking, funny, sausage, singleplayer, unlockables, stars, 2D, cute
